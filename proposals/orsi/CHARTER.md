# ORSi — Charter and boundaries

**Full name:** OperatoR System and Intelligence  
**Specification:** ORSi Spec  
**First profile:** ORSi-01, Device-Associated AI Service Entitlements  
**Mission:** For AI Development, Distribution & Sustainability  
**Status:** Review-ready preparation charter, **not adopted governance or a published standard**.

## Purpose

ORSi explores **interoperability without commercial uniformity**. It is open to **inference and model providers, AI application developers, customers**, and other voluntary adopters. No participant is required to use one model, device manufacturer, mobile operator, wallet, tariff or central platform.

The initial ORSi-01 profile explains *who issues a device-associated entitlement, which service/device it covers, when it applies, and whose commercial terms govern it*. It does not implement model inference, device authentication or payment settlement.

## Founding principles

1. **Provider autonomy:** prices, credits, talk-time packages, included routines, validity, recharge, support and billing remain each issuer's accepted commercial choice.
2. **Unit independence:** credits, actual inference, conversation time, licensed active-use hours, subscription period and result quality cannot be equated by ORSi.
3. **Customer choice and transparency:** material plans are understandable, provider-specific and reviewable. Unlimited covered routines are distinct from free remote inference or uncapped debt.
4. **Independent coexistence:** one compatible device may support multiple unrelated provider entitlements. One company's balance is not another company's account.
5. **Repeatable value:** a reviewed known task may be reused without fresh model inference, subject to permissions, verification and applicable subscription/service fees.
6. **Hardware and privacy honesty:** USB identifiers are not proof of genuine physical devices; cloud delivery is not permission to pool customer data; offline balances require appropriate threat-model disclosures.
7. **Progressive standardization:** define only small shared meanings now. Mark commercial choices `PROVIDER_DEFINED`, unused features `NOT_APPLICABLE` and unsupported future mechanisms `DEFERRED`.
8. **Voluntary adoption:** free/open and commercial offers may coexist. No compulsory commission, minimum tariff, central inference network, model license or revenue-sharing formula is created.

## Industry references and voluntary adoption — informative

**Inference/model suppliers** and **AI application developers** may independently build, distribute and maintain useful AI services under their own accepted commercial agreements. **Telecom operators** are referenced for familiar service-plan concepts (prepaid, postpaid, subscription and recharge); **flash-memory/USB manufacturers** are referenced for portable storage, provisioning and optional device-associated service designs. These technologies are useful examples, **not mandatory ORSi infrastructure**.

**Neither telecom companies nor flash-memory/USB manufacturers own, govern, control, veto or receive exclusive commercial rights over ORSi by virtue of their industry role.** Neither sector's participation is required for ORSi compatibility or governance. Their optional products and partnerships may be explored independently, without a compulsory royalty, sector fee or revenue share. This does not determine actual trademark, copyright or stewardship rights.

These are potential uses, **not verified adoption, endorsement or guaranteed profitability**. Deeper market cases remain in a separate non-normative workstream.

## Scope and exclusions

The first profile is limited to **device-associated AI service entitlements and the minimum accounting/disclosure vocabulary**. A USB may carry identity or data while inference runs on a host or remote provider. Future broader AI or “Any Intelligence” topics require separate proposals.

This charter does not prescribe a hardware form factor, USB controller, cryptographic design, license-key format, payment gateway, settlement network, trained model, implementation language or guaranteed anti-cloning/offline security.
