# ORSi Spec — ORSi-01: Device-Associated AI Service Entitlements

**Program:** ORSi — **OperatoR System and Intelligence**  
**Mission:** For AI Development, Distribution & Sustainability  
**Document class:** Proposed technical/commercial specification — first profile  
**Revision:** `2026-11-18-DRAFT`  
**Editorial maturity:** Review-ready preparation text; **not ratified, adopted, released or certified**  
**Status:** Candidate requirements below are proposals for review, not evidence of conforming implementation  
**Publication state:** This text is an unratified **review draft**. Its inclusion in `orsi-spec-project/operator-system-and-intelligence-spec` does not authorize a stable release, certification or publication announcement. The revision history remains in the preparation workstream.

## Abstract

ORSi-01 proposes a **small, provider-neutral language for AI service entitlements associated with a physical device**. It allows distinct companies to offer local or cloud-assisted services, sell and renew plans, account for use, and coexist on compatible devices without a central ORSi billing authority.

**Credits, conversation duration, inference resources, licensed active-use hours, plan validity and the usefulness of results are separate concepts.** ORSi does not prescribe a conversion, tariff, model runtime, inference protocol, hardware architecture, provider commission or universal unlimited plan.

The first profile defines **commercial and identity disclosure semantics**, not a licensing-key format, authentication algorithm, payment network or claim that portable storage alone executes inference.

## 1. Status and reading conventions

This text is a **preparation-stage proposal**. The words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD** and **MAY** describe proposed obligations *only within the stated applicability of a feature*. They have no binding status until a later specification release is accepted and published. **Informative** examples and sector opportunities do not create requirements.

Use these preparation statuses to prevent premature design commitments:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `CORE_CANDIDATE` | Minimal shared semantic proposed for a compatible device-associated offer |
| `PROVIDER_DEFINED` | The provider chooses an actual business or service policy and discloses material terms to the customer |
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | A feature is not part of the particular offer |
| `DEFERRED` | A possible future interoperable mechanism has not been standardized |

A draft may leave a decision `DEFERRED`. A real paid product MUST NOT treat a missing material term as customer consent, unlimited credit, free inference or permanent validity.

## 2. Scope and participants

**In scope:** Provider and service identity; an enrolled device reference and plan scope; entitlement state and validity; distinction among usage units; optional prepaid/postpaid/subscription/free plans; provider coexistence; transparent customer-facing charging and lifecycle information.

**Parties:**

- **Customer / subscriber:** Person or organization accepting a particular service offer.
- **Entitlement issuer:** Company accountable for granting and changing a service entitlement.
- **Billing provider:** Customer-facing merchant responsible for charges, refunds and support under that offer; MAY differ from the issuer or upstream supplier.
- **Inference supplier:** Company that performs model inference, directly for the customer or under an authorized business agreement.
- **Application / OperatoR System provider:** Company offering approved workflows, models, device experiences, maintenance or a permitted inference bundle.
- **Device manufacturer (optional):** Company supplying hardware if the service uses it. Neither a USB manufacturer nor a telecom operator is required to participate or govern ORSi; hardware production does not confer authority over inference charges.

A party MAY perform multiple roles, but customer-facing responsibility and authorized third-party rights must remain clear. **ORSi is open to inference/model providers, AI developers and other voluntary participants; no industry sector obtains ownership, governance authority, exclusive licensing, a veto or mandatory participation rights solely from its example role.** The device-associated scope of ORSi-01 does not mandate USB or telecom technology for future ORSi profiles.

## 3. Terminology

| Term | Proposed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Enrolled device** | The physical device or provider-scoped hardware reference identified in the plan |
| **Device reference** | Identifier within a declared namespace; **not** proof of genuine hardware or a secret |
| **Device scope** | One enrolled device, expressly authorized additional devices or a disclosed alternative |
| **Entitlement** | An issuer-specific authorization for a service under declared conditions |
| **Validity** | The applicable access period or explicitly declared absence of time expiry; where UTC interval timestamps are used, the start is inclusive and the end exclusive |
| **Provider credits** | Provider-defined commercial accounting units, not a universal currency or fixed minutes |
| **Talk time** | Measured or guaranteed conversation duration; not inherently a chargeable event |
| **Active-use hours** | Time that a provider chooses to measure as a separate licensed entitlement |
| **Usage receipt** | A provider-scoped, unique reference to an actual charged event or adjustment |
| **Covered workflow** | Activity expressly included in the customer's plan, possibly served by a reviewed reusable routine |
| **Fresh inference** | Model work actually performed by the identified inference service for a request |
| **Charging policy** | Versioned provider-specific rules for applying fees or credits to actual contracted use |

The term **independent edge AI** describes inference run locally or on dedicated edge compute; **shared cloud AI** describes a remote service infrastructure that may serve multiple customers. “Shared” does not authorize sharing customer data, memories or credit balances. A hybrid offer can use both.

## 4. Minimal shared entitlement description

The first ORSi-01 profile proposes only a **small common contract**. These are concepts, not a mandated JSON structure or transport schema.

| Concept | Status | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Issuer and service** | `CORE_CANDIDATE` | Who grants access and which service is covered |
| **Entitlement reference** | `CORE_CANDIDATE` | A unique reference within the issuer's declared scope |
| **Enrolled device and device scope** | `CORE_CANDIDATE` | Which device(s) the particular offer covers; provider-scoped references preferred for privacy |
| **Status and validity** | `CORE_CANDIDATE` | Whether access is active and what conditions/period govern it |
| **Published or accepted service terms** | `CORE_CANDIDATE` | Where the customer can inspect included activities, material exclusions and applicable commercial terms |
| **Billing party** | `CONDITIONAL` | Identified when charges exist, especially when different from issuer |
| **Meters, credit balances, talk-time allowance, receipts, cloud terms, renewal, device assurance** | `CONDITIONAL` | Meaningfully described only when the provider actually offers or claims the corresponding feature |

The provider MUST disclose material charging conditions and validity in a paid offer, even though ORSi does not prescribe their values. An implementation cannot claim that a feature is secure, unlimited, portable or free merely because an optional field is absent.

## 5. Provider autonomy and unit independence

Each customer-facing provider or independently contracted inference company **chooses its own** credit denomination, usage meter, prices, subscription periods, recharge, optional talk-time products and included work, subject to accepted agreements and applicable law.

ORSi MUST NOT introduce a mandatory 1:1, proportional or fixed conversion between **credits and talk time**. Two conversations lasting the same time can incur different model resource costs or provider-defined charges. Greater customer value may be achieved with fewer credits; ORSi does not define a quality score or guarantee that efficiency yields profit.

A provider MAY bill by inference request, model tier, task, consumed compute, separately purchased active conversation minutes, subscription access or other disclosed unit. It MAY offer free or subsidized service. If the provider sells a *guaranteed-minute product*, that guarantee applies to that offer, not other providers' credits.

**Validity, credits, active-use hours, talk-time allowance and subscription billing period do not automatically extend, restore or convert one another.** A recharge MUST follow its issuer's declared rules for added allowance and/or extended validity and MUST NOT silently replenish previously consumed units.

## 6. Optional single-device unlimited offers

A provider MAY sell **unlimited repetitions of clearly defined, covered workflows on one enrolled device** for the agreed validity period. This is a telecom-style *plan analogy*, not a compulsory ORSi offering.

The issuer defines the covered workflows, period, price, legitimate availability or fair-use conditions, supported execution hosts, maintenance, replacement/rebind and any explicitly authorized multi-device alternatives.

“Unlimited” MUST NOT silently imply free remote inference, all possible AI tasks, permanent validity, guaranteed compute capacity, exclusive ownership of the hardware, or unlimited financial liability. A plan offering unlimited covered local routines MAY coexist on the same hardware with another company's independently billed premium inference.

**Unlimited workflow repetitions**, **uncapped postpaid financial exposure**, and **an inference provider's capacity policy** are different choices. ORSi does not impose a hard spending ceiling. Providers MAY offer hard caps, alerts-only, or expressly authorized uncapped billing under disclosed terms, and may maintain lawful capacity, security or credit-risk controls.

## 7. Reusable routines and accurate charging

Previously reviewed, named and versioned workflows MAY be reused when applicable inputs, permissions and conditions remain valid. Before repeating work, an operator MAY check whether the desired state is already achieved. Fresh inference is not inherently required on every recurrence.

No provider should represent a locally reused or no-op routine as a **new inference event supplied by a third party** when none occurred. That does not invalidate a separately contracted subscription, maintenance, service-access, task or active-use-hours charge. Billing units, corrections and credits MUST correspond to the accepted commercial policy.

A provider MUST NOT double-charge the same event solely because of a retry or reconnection. Where credits are reserved before work, unspent reservation must be settled or released under the declared contract. Material charging-policy changes MUST follow applicable agreements and law rather than silently repricing completed use.

This profile does **not** prescribe how an application stores, trains, verifies or executes routines.

## 8. Independent providers and commercial roles

One enrolled physical device MAY support multiple independent entitlements. **Provider A MUST NOT be empowered by ORSi alone to debit, suspend, renew or overwrite Provider B's account, balance, service terms or customer data**.

An application provider may retail or bundle another company's inference only under appropriate rights and agreements. The **customer-facing price** and the **supplier's wholesale settlement** are separate contracts; no compulsory margin, royalty, shared wallet, marketplace or global clearing authority is defined.

A provider may choose exclusive terms for its own lawful product offering, but ORSi compatibility MUST NOT be described as a universal requirement that all providers share financial ledgers or surrender their independent accounts.

## 9. Edge, cloud, devices and assurance

A USB storage device may be a **portable carrier of configuration, model files or entitlement identity** while inference executes on a connected computer, edge box or cloud service. The issuer SHOULD disclose meaningful host dependencies, connectivity requirements, supported inference mode and material cost or privacy changes during cloud fallback.

A hardware **identifier**, proof of device **authenticity**, and protection of **freshness/integrity of changing credit state** are three separate properties. A public USB serial, vendor/product descriptor or ordinary hash MUST NOT be presented as cryptographic possession proof.

An implementation's offline accounting and clock trust cannot be inferred from this specification. Copied local state may be replayed, and network absence can delay revocation or reconciliation. Providers claiming offline entitlements SHOULD disclose their threat assumptions, limits, renewal and recovery behavior without promising rollback resistance they cannot establish.

## 10. Customer rights and lifecycle information

A provider offering a paid plan SHOULD make it practical for a customer to find the issuer/billing party, enrolled device, included benefits, valid period, actual charged units, status, renewal/recharge conditions, applicable rates, available alerts, dispute channel and important service limitations.

**Plan changes, support termination, upgrades, refunds, device loss, rebind, replacement, resale, cloud data transfers and provider exit** are governed by the provider's disclosed terms and applicable law. ORSi does not invent a universal refund promise or credit-portability right.

Cross-provider migrations, common authentication schemes, cryptographic key formats, payment APIs and detailed settlement protocols remain `DEFERRED` or outside the present profile. A missing mechanism must not be represented as an implemented capability.

## 11. Informative sector references and possible opportunities

**Telecom service plans and flash-memory/USB products are illustrative references only.** Neither industry owns, controls or governs ORSi, and neither must sponsor, manufacture or distribute an ORSi-compatible service. This does not determine formal trademark ownership, contributor rights or a future governance arrangement.

The following are **optional opportunities**, not ORSi requirements, verified business outcomes, endorsements or guaranteed new revenue:

| Segment | Optional opportunity under ORSi-compatible offerings |
| --- | --- |
| **Model publishers and inference companies** | Independently distribute models and charge for genuine service under their own licences, credits and accepted terms |
| **AI application / system providers** | Offer approved reusable workflows, edge/cloud services, upgrades and customer support under independent agreements |
| **Telecom operators (reference only)** | Illustrate prepaid/postpaid, validity, recharge and service-bundle approaches; optional distribution relationships |
| **Flash-memory / USB manufacturers (reference only)** | Illustrate portable entitlement carriers, provisioning and lifecycle support; optional verified device-assurance offerings |
| **Customers** | Compare eligible benefits, validity and service terms while selecting multiple compatible offerings; reuse appropriate work without needless inference |

The telecom analogy concerns **plan composition and operator independence**; it does not adopt telecom regulations, SIM/eSIM security, roaming tariffs or number portability. Flash storage standards address storage devices and their properties, **not an ORSi licensing or authentication guarantee**. Deeper industry cases belong in a separately maintained, non-normative case-studies workstream, not this first specification profile.

## 12. Profile evaluation and evolution

This preparation draft proposes the following review expectations, **not a certified conformance test**:

1. A reviewer can identify the issuer, service, covered device scope, status, validity and customer-facing terms.
2. Optional billed units can be distinguished; provider credits never imply a universal talk-time exchange.
3. An unlimited offer identifies included activities and does not silently include premium inference or uncapped debt.
4. Multiple providers retain independent accounts; an issuer cannot rewrite another provider's credits merely through ORSi compatibility.
5. Claimed hardware, offline and cloud properties are documented without presenting a serial number as authentication.
6. Actual provider charges, corrections and renewal terms can be reconciled without silent duplicate usage.

The proposed [written scenarios](CONFORMANCE-SCENARIOS.md) are discussion cases marked **NOT_RUN**. Additional rules should be considered **only for demonstrated customer/provider needs** through the [progressive specification process](../governance/PROGRESSIVE-SPECIFICATION.md), with security, backward compatibility and consumer implications reviewed.

## 13. Out of scope and publication controls

ORSi-01 does **not** define AI model runtimes, agent command interfaces, MCP servers, data formats, device firmware, USB manufacturing requirements, cryptographic algorithms, token encodings, payment gateways, merchant settlements, a mandatory licensing tax, a central authority or a legal certification.

**Do not claim ORSi-01 conformance to a released specification**, publish it as current, or change the separate future implementation repository on the basis of this draft. Finalization requires documented stakeholder acceptance, licensing/trademark and governance checks, and a **separate explicit authorization** before publication in `operator-system-and-intelligence-spec`.
